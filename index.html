Behind the curtain

About

Paul Trott studied lighting design at WAAPA between 1995 and 1996, living in Mt Lawley ECU student housing for most of that time. In 1998, he studied graphic design and, after graduating, began working as a 3D animator for television commercials.

In his spare time, Paul taught himself web design, starting a new journey as a web designer in 2000.

Following a decade as a designer and web developer, Paul was introduced to Bitcoin in October 2010. He purchased 5,000 BTC for around $22 AUD, which he later sold in 2018 for over $80 million. Paul then retired, had two children, and moved to a coastal town on the southern coast of Australia.

Now living in his own converted studio lighthouse—seated on the cliffs and overlooking the ocean—he spends his time crafting limited-edition designer toys, drinking Yorkshire tea, and listening to great music.

Current project: A plastik robot.

Loves: Yorkshire tea, sports mixtures, science fiction, a crisp grid line, eink, fiskars desk mat.

Dislikes: Over ripe bananas, pips, purple, eating ASMR

Favourite Animal: Elephant shrew
